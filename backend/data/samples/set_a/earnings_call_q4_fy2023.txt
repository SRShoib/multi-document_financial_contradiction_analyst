ACME CORPORATION
Q4 AND FULL YEAR 2023 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL TRANSCRIPT
January 30, 2024

OPERATOR: Good afternoon and welcome to ACME Corporation's fourth quarter and full year 2023 earnings call.

CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER: Thank you. We are pleased with our performance this year. Full year revenue came in strong and our Cloud Services segment performed well.

CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER: Turning to our outlook. For full fiscal year 2024, we now expect total revenue to be between $1,300 million and $1,350 million. This reflects a more cautious view of enterprise spending in the first half of the year than we had previously communicated.

On a separate note, I want to address a legal matter. We are currently a party to a material lawsuit relating to a commercial dispute. While we intend to defend ourselves vigorously, an adverse outcome could have a significant adverse effect on our results of operations.

ANALYST: Thank you. Can you give more color on the revised revenue outlook for 2024?

CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER: Certainly. The revised range reflects softer demand signals we observed late in the fourth quarter.
