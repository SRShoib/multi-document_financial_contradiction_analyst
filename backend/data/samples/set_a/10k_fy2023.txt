ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Total revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023 was $1,000.0 million, an increase of 8% compared to the prior fiscal year. The increase was driven primarily by growth in our Cloud Services segment. Net income for fiscal year 2023 was $120.0 million, compared to $110.0 million in fiscal year 2022. Gross margin was 62% for fiscal year 2023.

Operating expenses for fiscal year 2023 were $520.0 million. Research and development expense was $180.0 million, representing 18% of total revenue.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Our business is subject to numerous risks, including competition, technological change, and macroeconomic conditions that could adversely affect demand for our products.

ITEM 3. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

We are not currently a party to any pending material legal proceedings, and we do not anticipate any material litigation that would have a significant adverse effect on our business, financial condition, or results of operations.

OUTLOOK

For fiscal year 2024, we currently expect total revenue to grow in the low double digits compared to fiscal year 2023.
