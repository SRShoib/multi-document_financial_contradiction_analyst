ACME CORPORATION
FORM 10-Q
QUARTERLY REPORT FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2024

ITEM 1. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

Total revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2024 was $310.0 million, an increase of 6% compared to the first quarter of fiscal year 2023. Net income for the first quarter of fiscal year 2024 was $30.0 million. Gross margin was 61% for the quarter.

ITEM 2. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Revenue growth in the first quarter was driven by our Cloud Services segment, partially offset by softer demand in our Legacy Hardware segment.
