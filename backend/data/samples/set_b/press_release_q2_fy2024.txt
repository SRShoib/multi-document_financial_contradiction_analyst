GLOBEX CORPORATION REPORTS SECOND QUARTER FISCAL 2024 RESULTS

July 25, 2024 — Globex Corporation today announced results for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024.

Revenue for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024 was $500 million, up 11% year over year. Net income for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024 was $60 million.

GUIDANCE

For full fiscal year 2024, Globex currently expects total revenue of approximately $1,800 million.
