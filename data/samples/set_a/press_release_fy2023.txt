ACME CORPORATION REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2023 RESULTS

January 30, 2024 — ACME Corporation today announced financial results for its fourth quarter and full fiscal year ended December 31, 2023.

Full year 2023 total revenue was $1,200 million, a record result for the company and an increase of 8% over fiscal 2022. Net income for the full year was $120 million. The company delivered a gross margin of 62% for the year.

"We are proud of our record revenue performance in 2023," said the Chief Executive Officer. "Our Cloud Services segment continues to lead our growth."

GUIDANCE

For full fiscal year 2024, ACME currently expects total revenue of approximately $1,400 million, reflecting continued strong demand across all segments.

The company will host a conference call to discuss these results.
