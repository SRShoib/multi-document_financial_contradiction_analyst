INITECH CORPORATION REPORTS FULL YEAR 2025 RESULTS

January 28, 2026 — Initech Corporation today announced results for fiscal year 2025.

Full year fiscal 2025 revenue was $2,000 million. Net income for fiscal year 2025 was $250 million. The company reported a gross margin of 60% for the year.

The company is not currently a party to any pending material legal proceedings.
