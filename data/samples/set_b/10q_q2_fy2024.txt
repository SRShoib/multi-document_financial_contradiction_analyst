GLOBEX CORPORATION
FORM 10-Q
QUARTERLY REPORT FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024

ITEM 1. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

Total revenue for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024 was $450.0 million. Net income for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024 was $60.0 million. Gross margin was 57% for the quarter.

ITEM 2. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Revenue in the second quarter was affected by softer demand in the Industrial Automation segment.
